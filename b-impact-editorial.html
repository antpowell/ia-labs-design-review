Hands-on STEM for the kids who need it most.

IA LABS brings robotics, STEM kits, and a mobile maker space to underserved communities across Houston — free programs for grades 4–10, built and taught by veterans.

We don't lecture.
We build.

Every IA LABS session starts with a pile of parts and ends with something that spins, lights up, or rolls. Kids learn engineering the way engineers do — by making a mess, making it work, and making it theirs.
